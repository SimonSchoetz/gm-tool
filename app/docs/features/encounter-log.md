# Encounter log

**Purpose.** The encounter sidebar's Log tab lists the fight's combat log with times, reverts automatic entries and takes typed messages with @mentions of entities. The Data, Open points and Where to look sections for the whole encounter screen are on [Encounter queue](encounter-queue.md).

**Users.** The GM, as everywhere in the app.

## How it works

Happy path:

1. The Log tab lists every entry of the encounter's combat log, oldest at the top. It opens only on a click on its tab: an action, a selection or an Add never switches to it. The list is scrolled to the newest entry when the tab opens and whenever a new entry appears, and with no entries it reads "No entries yet.". Every entry shows the time it was made at its right, as hour and minute in 12-hour format ("2:32 PM"), without a date.
2. An automatic entry is a compact muted line that reads its label first and then every target its entry holds, comma-separated, each with its amount or value in brackets: "Added: Goblin ×5" ("Added: Goblin" for one, the name without its number), "Damage: Goblin 3 (5)", "Heal: Goblin 3 (4)", "Temp HP: Aria (5)", "Max HP: Goblin 3 (12)", "Defeated: Goblin 3", "Undefeated: Goblin 3", "Removed: Goblin 3" and "Reverted: Damage: Goblin 3 (5)". A line names a combatant as the queue shows it now, so a rename, an entity rename or a number dropped after a sibling's removal changes how earlier lines read. A removed combatant is named as if it were in the queue beside the remaining combatants of its key, so it reads "Goblin 2" while the queue shows "Goblin"; when no sibling of its key remains, it is numbered among every combatant of its key, so two removed Goblins read "Goblin 1" and "Goblin 2".
3. While the pointer is over a revertible entry (Damage, Heal, Temp HP, Max HP, Defeated, Undefeated or Removed) that is not yet reverted, a Revert icon (an undo arrow, with the tooltip "Revert") shows; one click writes a `revert` entry at once, without a confirmation. The fight's values become what they would be had the entry never happened, and every later entry stays in effect: a combatant at `7/7` with Damage 5 then Heal 4 shows `6/7`, and reverting the Damage shows `7/7`. The reverted entry stays in the list, struck through and muted, without a Revert icon, and a "Reverted: …" line is added at the bottom. An entry is reverted once; `Added` entries, `Reverted` lines and typed messages have no Revert. A revert drops the whole entry, every target it lists included. Reverting a Removed entry brings every listed combatant back into the queue at its old place with the HP, defeated mark and number the remaining entries give, and none of them is selected.
4. A composer sits at the bottom of the Log tab with the placeholder "Write a message...". It takes text, line breaks and @mentions of entities, as the notes editor's @ list offers them, and has no formatting toolbar, slash commands, lists or headings; a combatant such as "Goblin 3" is plain text, only its entity can be mentioned. Enter sends the message as a `message` entry, Shift+Enter starts a new line, and Enter while the @ list is open picks the highlighted entity and sends nothing. An empty message, or one of spaces only, is not sent, and a message that holds only a mention is. After a send the composer is empty, and the message is the newest entry, shown as a chat bubble in normal text colour with its mentions as the notes editor shows them; a click on a mention opens the entity's mention popup. A refused send keeps the typed text.
5. While the pointer is over a typed message, a Delete icon (a trash icon, with the tooltip "Delete") shows; one click removes the message at once, without a confirmation. A message cannot be edited, and an automatic entry cannot be deleted. In the 200 px sidebar a message's text takes the whole width of its entry, with the Delete icon and the time stamp right-aligned on a second line below it, and an unbroken message wraps inside the column.

Failure:

- A log entry that cannot be read — an unknown kind, invalid JSON or a payload that fails its schema — applies nothing and never stops the others, a target without a queue item is skipped, and the Log tab shows the entry as "Unreadable entry" with its time and no Revert.
- While a log write of the encounter is pending — an action, a Revert, a Delete or a send — every action button, every Revert and Delete icon and sending are blocked: the buttons and icons are disabled until the queue shows the new numbers, and a second call made in the same moment is refused and writes nothing. A refused send keeps the typed text.
- The Log tab leaves out a target that has no queue item on this device and shows only the label when none is left, and it shows a `Reverted` entry whose target the derivation ignored as "Unreadable entry"; both arise only mid-sync and fix themselves once the sync completes, and neither throws.

Where the user waits: the Log tab shows the loading icon in its list area until the queue, the log and the entities have loaded, and its composer is usable at once.
