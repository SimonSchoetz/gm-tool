# Toggle editing

**Purpose.** The GM writes notes in the app's document text editor and uses toggles to fold parts of them away. Enter, Shift+Enter, Backspace, the mention and slash-command menus and paste behave in a toggle's header the way they do in any other block, so text is never lost, no header line becomes impossible to delete, and new blocks and mentions can be added inside a toggle.

## How it works

In the editable document text editor of a content section (the read-only editor of a mention popup takes no key or paste, and the one-line message editor has no toggles), a toggle is one header block, a paragraph or a heading, and one body of blocks. The header always stays one block: whatever would make a second block there goes to where Enter at the end of the header already leads, the start of the body of an open toggle, or directly after a collapsed toggle.

- **A menu is open.** With the mention menu or the slash-command menu open in a header, Enter picks the highlighted entry and does nothing else: no line is added, the caret stays in the header and no block is created.
- **Enter, no menu.** At the end of the header of an open toggle the caret moves to the start of the body's first block, and at the end of a collapsed toggle's header an empty paragraph appears directly after the toggle with the caret in it, the toggle staying collapsed. This holds whatever the header ends with: plain text, formatted text, a soft line break or a mention badge. In the middle of the header, the text before the caret stays in the header and the text after it moves into a new block with the caret at its start, at the start of the body or directly after a collapsed toggle; a heading header gives a heading of the same level. At the very start of a header that holds text, an empty paragraph is added directly above the toggle and the header and caret stay as they were. With text selected inside the header, Enter first removes the selected text and then acts at that caret; any other selection keeps the editor's own Enter.
- **Shift+Enter** anywhere in the header, its end included, inserts a soft line break; the header stays one header and the caret stays in it.
- **Backspace.** At the start of a header's second or later line it joins that line to the line above and leaves the toggle and its body alone. At the very start of the header's first line it turns the toggle into plain blocks: the header becomes a normal paragraph (a heading stays a heading of its level), the body's blocks follow directly below it at the toggle's own level in their order, and the caret stays at the start of the former header text. Nothing is deleted, an empty block included, and a toggle inside the body stays a toggle.
- **Paste.** Pasting several blocks with the caret in a header puts the first block's text into the header at the caret and the other blocks where Enter would put them, in their order; the header text that followed the caret ends up at the end of the last pasted block. When the first pasted block is a list, a table or a toggle, the header keeps only the text before the caret and that block goes to the Enter destination; when the last is one, the header text that followed the caret becomes a paragraph of its own right after the pasted blocks. A single pasted paragraph, a paste outside a header and a paste over a selection that reaches out of the header are left to the editor. Copying blocks above a toggle together with the toggle and pasting them into a plain paragraph puts the blocks above the pasted toggle and the toggle in whole, header, body and open or collapsed state as copied.
- **Repair.** A toggle saved with extra blocks between its header and its body shows, once the text is opened, the first of them as its header and every further one at the start of its body, in order. The same repair runs after any edit that would leave a toggle with another shape than one header and one body; blocks found after the body go to the end of the body and a second body's blocks join the first, in order. No text is deleted.

Failure: nothing in this feature can fail visibly; a paste or key that the feature does not take is handled by the editor as it always was. Where the user waits: nowhere.

## Data

- **Reads:** the editor's document, its toggles and the caret or selection in it; the clipboard on a paste.
- **Writes:** the same document, through the editor's own save, as any other edit.

## Where to look

- Keys and menus: `ToggleKeyboardPlugin` with its helpers `isCaretAtBlockEnd`, `isCaretAtBlockStart`, `unwrapToggle` and `resolveHeaderToggleForRemoval`, and `splitHeaderAtSelection` in the text editor's helper folder.
- The one-header-one-body repair: `ToggleHeaderGuardPlugin` and its helper `normalizeToggleChildren`.
- Paste: `TogglePastePlugin` with its helpers `insertBlocksBehindHeader` and `plainTextToBlocks`.
- The toggle's saved shape: the `ToggleNode` and `ToggleBodyNode` of the text editor.
- Tests: `ToggleKeyboardPlugin.test`, `isCaretAtBlockEnd.test`, `isCaretAtBlockStart.test`, `splitHeaderAtSelection.test`, `unwrapToggle.test`, `ToggleHeaderGuardPlugin.test`, `normalizeToggleChildren.test`, `TogglePastePlugin.test`, `insertBlocksBehindHeader.test`, `plainTextToBlocks.test`; their shared editor fixtures sit in the toggle plugins' test support folder.
