# Encounter queue

**Purpose.** The encounter screen holds a queue of combatants and events that the GM adds from the adventure's base entities or types in, each combatant showing its image and HP with its numbered name as the entry's tooltip, so the GM sets up a fight's order and HP on the encounter itself and runs the fight there, instead of tracking it outside the app. Duplicate copies the queue at full HP for another fight. The encounter's cover keeps its map at hand on the screen and makes each encounter recognisable in the encounters list, and wherever an entity has no image a placeholder icon fits its domain. The screen's documentation is split by area: this page covers the screen's layout, the queue, adding (entities, random combatants, events), Hide defeated and Duplicate, and holds the Data, Open points and Where to look sections for the whole screen; [Encounter selection and actions](encounter-selection-and-actions.md) covers selecting queue entries and the Actions tab, [Encounter log](encounter-log.md) the Log tab, and [Encounter shown item](encounter-shown-item.md) the middle column's view of the clicked entry.

**Users.** The GM, as everywhere in the app.

## How it works

Happy path:

1. The encounter screen shows three columns. The left column is 200 px wide: it starts with the encounter's cover, a 200 × 200 image upload, and holds the queue below it, with Hide defeated, Duplicate and Delete below the queue. The middle column holds the global combat notes at the top, in a panel as high as the cover (200 px) so the two line up, and, below them, the shown item's view. The right column is a sidebar, 200 px wide like the left column, with an Add tab, an Actions tab and a Log tab, drawn like browser tabs; the Add tab is open on its Entities switch when the encounter opens, and the middle column takes the width that remains.
2. Under the Add tab's Entities switch, the tab lists the adventure's base entities of every type by name, A to Z, each row with the entity's image (the placeholder icon of its type when it has none: a gem for an item, a castle for a location, a flag for a faction, the person silhouette for an NPC, a PC and a foe), its name, its type label and a plus icon with the tooltip "Add". Six boxes (NPC, Foe, PC, Faction, Location, Item) filter the list to the checked types, and none checked lists every type. The choice is saved on this device and survives leaving the encounter, opening another encounter and restarting the app. The search box narrows the list by name and description, name matches first.
3. A click on the plus icon puts one combatant of the entity at the end of the queue. There is no quantity field, so a second combatant is a second click. While an Add of the encounter is pending, every plus icon of the Add tab is disabled.
4. A combatant shows the entity's name. While an entity has one combatant in the encounter it reads "Goblin"; once a second exists both are numbered ("Goblin 1", "Goblin 2"), and further adds continue from the highest number the entity has had in the encounter, removed combatants included. A removed combatant does not count towards the numbering of the others, so removing "Goblin 2" leaves the other one reading "Goblin" and the next Add shows "Goblin 1" and "Goblin 3". A combatant of an unnamed entity reads "Untitled Combatant". A combatant the GM renamed shows its own name instead ([Encounter shown item](encounter-shown-item.md), step 2).
5. When the entity has exactly one stat block section (Basic or 5e) whose HP is a number, each new combatant starts with that number as its max and current HP, copied at the moment of the add. A queue entry shows `current/max`, followed by `+temp` when temp HP is above 0, and the entity's image beside them.
6. A combatant that has lost HP has its image tinted red from the bottom up, covering the share of max HP that is missing; at current HP 0 or below, or at max HP 0 or below, the image turns grey instead. A defeated combatant's image is grey whatever its HP, in place of the red tint, and unmarking brings back its colours and the tint its HP gives; the entry is not dimmed. A combatant whose HP is not set shows no HP text and no tint.
7. Dragging a queue entry by its handle moves it to the drop position among the shown entries, and the new order survives leaving the encounter and restarting the app. A selected entry moves with the whole selection as one block, keeping the order of its members; an unselected entry moves alone and leaves the selection unchanged. While a block is held, the other selected entries disappear from their places and the dragged entry shows a "+N" badge, N the number of other selected entries; on release all of them land together. The entry, or the block, lands directly before or after the shown entry it is dropped on, and every hidden or removed combatant keeps its order relative to the entries that did not move, so a hidden one reappears among them as it was. A drag that is cancelled, or dropped where it started, changes nothing.
8. Renaming a base entity changes the shown name of its combatants in every encounter, except a combatant the GM renamed, which keeps its own name. Deleting a base entity keeps its combatants, shown with the name the entity had when they were added, or their own name when the GM renamed them, and the image placeholder.
9. Each Add also writes one `added` entry to the encounter's combat log naming the queue items it added.
10. A Hide defeated box under the queue, above Duplicate and Delete, hides defeated combatants while it is checked. A hidden combatant keeps its place and reappears there when the box is unchecked. The screen changes on the click and the write follows about 500 ms later.
11. The Add tab starts with a switch "Entities" | "Other", drawn as the session screen's Prep / In-game switch. "Entities" shows the type filter, the search and the entity list of steps 2 and 3. "Other" shows a second switch "Event" | "Random Combatant" with the form of the chosen one. Opening the encounter starts on "Entities", and "Other" starts on "Event". The choice stays while the encounter stays open, switching to the Actions or Log tab and back included, and is not saved; the typed name and HP of the forms and the entity search text stay through every switch too.
12. "Random Combatant" shows a name field with the placeholder "Name", an HP field with the placeholder "HP", which may stay empty, and a plus icon with the tooltip "Add", in one row with the HP field. The plus icon is disabled while the name is empty or spaces only and while the HP field holds anything but empty or a whole number of 0 or more (`1e3` counts as 1000, as in the amount fields). Enter in one of the fields adds when the plus icon is enabled. A click on it puts one random combatant at the end of the queue, with the typed name trimmed and no entity: with an HP, it starts at that max and current HP; with an empty HP field, its HP is not set. After the Add the name and HP fields are empty.
13. "Event" shows a name field with the placeholder "Name" and a plus icon with the tooltip "Add", in one row, disabled while the name is empty or spaces only. Enter adds when the plus icon is enabled. A click on it puts one event, its name trimmed, at the end of the queue and empties the field.
14. Each of these Adds writes one `added` entry, as an entity Add does: "Added: Grunt", "Added: Lair action" for an event; an entry naming several queue items shows their count ("Added: Grunt ×3"). Every Add, the entity Add included, refreshes the log, so its line shows in the Log tab at once. Log lines name random combatants and events by the name the queue shows now.
15. Random combatants are numbered by their exact name, letter case included, as entity combatants are numbered by their entity: one "Grunt" reads "Grunt"; once a second exists they read "Grunt 1" and "Grunt 2"; further adds continue from the highest number that name has had in the encounter, removed combatants included, and removed combatants do not count towards the others' numbers. A combatant whose entity was deleted counts with the random combatants of its stored name. A renamed random combatant shows its own name without a number and still counts for its siblings. Events are never numbered and never count towards a combatant's number: an event "Grunt" and a random combatant "Grunt" read "Grunt" each.
16. A random combatant's queue entry shows the person silhouette as its image placeholder and its HP, with its shown name as the entry's tooltip and the red tint, grey and defeated look of entity combatants.
17. An event's queue entry is as tall as a combatant's: an icon tile the size of the combatant image, showing an event icon, stands in the image's place, followed by the event's name. It has no HP text and no tint, and Hide defeated never hides an event.
18. Events take part in selection (click, Cmd/Ctrl-click, Shift-click ranges), in the drag of one entry or of a selected block, and in the saved order, as combatants do. A click on an event moves the Add tab to Actions, as a click on a combatant does.
19. Duplicate creates a new encounter named as before, with the same global notes and Hide defeated setting, and a copy of every queue item the source has not removed: entity combatants, random combatants and events, in the same order, with the same names, renames, numbers and notes. Each copied combatant starts at full HP: max HP as the source shows it at that moment, current HP equal to it, no temp HP, not defeated; a combatant whose HP is not set stays not set, and a defeated combatant hidden by Hide defeated is copied too, not defeated. The copy's log is empty. Opening the copy starts with nothing selected and nothing shown and the Add tab on "Entities" and "Event" with empty fields, and its next Add continues numbering from the highest number in the copy.
20. The first item of the encounter screen's left column is the encounter's cover, a 200 × 200 image upload that works as the base entity screen's sidebar image does: with no cover, a click on it opens the file picker; once an image is chosen, the image shows at 200 × 200, and a click on the shown image opens the image viewer dialog, where the image can be replaced or removed. Removing it puts the empty upload control back. The cover is saved with the encounter's other edits, and it is still there after leaving and reopening the encounter and after an app restart.
21. In the 200 px column a queue entry shows its handle at the entry's full height, its 64 px image and its HP; its shown name is the entry's tooltip, and nothing overflows the column horizontally.
22. The encounters list shows a "Cover" column first: 136 px wide, not sortable and not resizable, with the encounter's cover in each row, or a crossed-swords icon when it has none. The image column of every other table list (PCs, NPCs, Factions, Locations, Foes, Items) reads "Cover" too.
23. An entity with no image shows the icon of its domain wherever an image placeholder shows: in the table lists' Cover column, the queue rows and the Add tab's rows. An encounter shows crossed swords, an item a gem, a location a castle, a faction a flag; a PC, an NPC and a foe keep the person silhouette, and so does a random combatant, which has no domain.
24. Duplicating an encounter that has a cover gives the copy its own copy of the cover image, and removing the cover from either encounter leaves the other's in place. Deleting an encounter deletes its cover image with it. Deleting an adventure deletes the image files of its base entities and the covers of its encounters, besides its own cover, and no image of another adventure.

Failure:

- While an Add of the encounter is pending, every plus icon of the Add tab is disabled, those of the Event and Random Combatant forms included; a second Add call made in the same moment is refused, and a refused Add leaves the typed name and HP as they were.
- A quit within about 500 ms of clicking Hide defeated loses that one toggle, since it saves through the encounter's autosave.
- A Duplicate within about 500 ms of the last keystroke of a rename or note copies the stored state without that edit; the source keeps the edit.
- Duplicate writes the new encounter and then its queue items one by one. When a write fails part-way, the new encounter is deleted again with whatever of its queue was already written, and the error screen shows: no copy remains. The copy of the cover image is deleted with it. An app quit part-way through leaves the new encounter with part of its queue.
- If the `added` log entry cannot be written after the item was stored, the Add still counts as done and the error is swallowed, so a retry cannot add the same item twice. The log entry is then missing.
- Deleting an encounter or an adventure deletes the row first and its images after it. An image that cannot be deleted then stays on disk with nothing naming it until the app next starts and deletes it, and the delete still counts as done. A failure before the row is gone leaves the encounter or adventure with every image it had.
- Replacing or removing a cover points the encounter at the new cover, or at none, before the old image is deleted. An old image that cannot be deleted stays on disk with nothing naming it until the app next starts and deletes it, and the change still counts as done. A failure before that leaves the encounter on its old cover.
- A failing read or write of the queue or the log shows the app's error screen, as everywhere in the app.
- An adventure without base entities shows "This adventure has no entities yet." in the Add tab; a search without a match shows "No results found".

Where the user waits: the encounter screen opens once its cover image has loaded, so the cover does not pop in, and an encounter with no cover waits for nothing; a chosen cover shows once its save has finished, the write following about 500 ms after the choice, and a removed cover disappears once the removal has finished; during a block drag the other selected entries leave the list one frame after the drag starts; the Add tab's Entities panel shows the loading icon until the adventure's entities have loaded, while the Entities / Other switch and the Event and Random Combatant forms never wait; the screen shows the loading icon while the encounter loads. A drag reorder shows its new order at once, ahead of the write.

## Data

- **Reads:** the encounter's queue items; the adventure's base entities of all six types, from the same cached lists the entity list screens use, so any change to an entity refreshes the queue and the Add tab; the encounter's log entries, oldest first, which the Log tab lists and the queue is derived from; the encounter's `hide_defeated`; an entity's content sections, at the moment of an Add for the stat block HP and while a combatant is shown for the HP-not-set sentence and for the shown combatant's read-only entity content, which reads the entity's image and sections and writes nothing; the per-device setting `encounterAddFilter`; for Duplicate, the source encounter's queue items and log entries, from which each copied item's max HP is derived.
- **Writes:** one queue item per Add, with the encounter, the kind `combatant`, the entity, its name at that time, its sequence number, its starting max HP when the stat block gave one, and its position in the queue; one random combatant per Add, with the kind `combatant`, no entity, the typed `name` trimmed, its `sequence_number` among the combatants of that name, its `starting_max_hp` when an HP was typed, and its position; one event per Add, with the kind `event`, the typed `name` trimmed, no entity, no sequence number and its position; one `added` entry in the combat log, its payload the list of queue item ids; one log entry per action, its kind and its payload, which lists every target the action changed (`damage` and `heal` store `targets` of queue item id and `amount`, `temp-hp` and `max-hp` store `targets` of queue item id and `value`, `defeated`, `undefeated` and `removed` store `queueItemIds`), and the entries the Log tab writes: a `revert` entry per Revert, which stores the `entryId` it reverts, and a `message` entry per send, which stores the `editorState` (the serialised editor state) of the typed message; the deletion of a typed message, which removes its log row and is limited to `message` rows, so the rest of the log is never deleted; the encounter's `hide_defeated`; the rename of a combatant (`custom_name`), the rename of an event (its `name`, null when emptied) and the notes of a queue item, each through the autosave path with no log entry; a new position for every queue item on a reorder, the hidden and removed ones keeping their places; the setting `encounterAddFilter`, a list of entity types; for Duplicate, a copy of every queue item the source has not removed, with a new id, the new encounter and every other column as stored, except `starting_max_hp`, which takes the max HP derived from the source's log at that moment (null for HP not set and for an event); no log entry.
- **Cover:** the encounter's `image_id`, a nullable reference to an image row that becomes empty when the image row is deleted. A chosen file is stored as a new image, or as the replacement of the old one, and its id written to `image_id`; removing the cover deletes the image and empties the column; Duplicate copies the image into a new row for the copy; deleting the encounter or its adventure deletes the image. The migration `add_encounter_cover_image` adds the column, sets the `label` of the image column of the six base-entity lists to Cover and prepends the Cover column to the encounters list's layout, keeping every width and sort state and leaving the `table_config` rows' `updated_at` as it was. The column syncs with the encounter row, as the sync registry takes every column of the table's schema; the migration gives the build a new migration head.
- **Columns with no reader yet:** none. The kind `event` on the queue items is written by the event Add and read by the naming, the queue, the Actions tab and the middle column.
- **Sync:** the queue items and the log entries sync to paired devices as other tables do, listed after the encounters, random combatants, events and the copied queue of a duplicated encounter included. The setting is not synced. The copy of a duplicated encounter takes the source's `hide_defeated`. A deleted typed message syncs as a deletion, so it disappears from a paired device. Deleting an encounter deletes its queue items and log entries; deleting a base entity empties the entity link of its queue items.
- **Derived, not stored:** a combatant's max, current and temp HP, its defeated mark and its removal are derived from its queue item and the encounter's log entries, so no resulting value is stored, and which entries a revert dropped are derived the same way; the shown name is derived from the live entity, and from `custom_name` when it is set.

## Open points

None in the open-questions register.

A paired device on a build without the migrations `add_encounter_queue_and_log` and `add_encounter_cover_image` pauses sync with a build that has them until both run the same build: a peer counts as compatible only when its sync protocol version and its migration head both equal the device's own (`syncService`). The sync protocol version is 3.
