# Encounter shown item

**Purpose.** The encounter screen's middle column shows the clicked queue entry below the global combat notes: a combatant with its editable combat name, read-only HP, own notes and its entity's mention popup content, read-only (the section header row, the image and the selected section's body), or an event with its name and notes. The Data, Open points and Where to look sections for the whole encounter screen are on [Encounter queue](encounter-queue.md).

**Users.** The GM, as everywhere in the app.

## How it works

Happy path:

1. With nothing shown, the middle column reads "Click an entry in the queue to show it here." below the global notes.
2. With a combatant shown, the middle column below the global notes starts with a name field showing the name the queue shows ("Goblin 3"). Typing another name renames this combatant only: the queue entry, its Actions tab row and every Log line naming that one combatant show the typed name, without a number, instantly as the GM types, and the database write follows about 0.5 s later. The "Added" line keeps the entity's name ("Added: Goblin ×5"). Emptying the field, a name of spaces only, or typing exactly the automatic name returns the combatant to its automatic name, numbered as before. Leaving the field or pressing Enter restores the shown name in the field. A renamed combatant still counts for its siblings' numbers: renaming "Goblin 1" to "Boss" leaves the other showing "Goblin 2". A rename writes no log entry.
3. Below the name the HP shows read-only as the queue shows it (`4/7`, `4/7 +2`), followed by a muted "Defeated" label while the combatant is marked defeated. It changes only through the Actions tab. A combatant whose HP is not set shows a sentence instead: "HP is not set: the entity has no stat block with an HP value. Set max HP in the Actions tab." when its entity has no stat block section or its one stat block has an empty HP; "HP is not set: the entity has more than one stat block. Set max HP in the Actions tab." when it has two or more; "HP is not set. Set max HP in the Actions tab." otherwise, when the stat block changed after the add or the entity was deleted. The entity's sections are read now, not at the moment of the add, and while they load a linked combatant shows no sentence.
4. Below the HP a notes editor with the placeholder "Combatant notes..." takes text and @mentions as the app's other notes editors do. Its text belongs to this combatant only: Goblin 3's notes are not Goblin 1's and do not appear on the Goblin entity. A note writes no log entry.
5. Below the notes, a combatant whose entity exists shows its entity's mention popup content, read-only, in one panel: the header row with the selected section's name, the section menu toggle and the up/down arrows, shown only when the entity has more than one section and never with the popup's entity name, Navigate, Pin or Close; the entity's image beside the section, when it has one; and the selected section's body as the popup shows it (a 5e stat block hides its empty fields). Stepping or picking a section from the menu changes the shown section. Nothing of the entity can be edited here and its description is not shown. A click on the image shows it full screen, read-only: only a title and Close, no replace or remove control; Close, Escape or a click on the backdrop closes it, and the combatant stays shown and selected. A combatant whose entity was deleted shows its name, HP, defeated label and notes, and no entity content.
6. The global notes keep their fixed-height panel at the top. Everything below it, from the name field to the end of the entity content, scrolls as one area: the combatant notes grow with their text, and the entity content fits the column's width, so a narrow window squeezes the section's region instead of widening the column.
7. A shown random combatant shows its name field ("Combatant name"), its HP and "Defeated" label and its notes editor ("Combatant notes..."), with no entity content; with HP not set it reads "HP is not set. Set max HP in the Actions tab.". A shown event shows a name field labelled "Event name" with the event's name and a notes editor with the placeholder "Event notes..." that takes text and @mentions, with no HP and no entity view. Typing renames the event in the queue and in every log line naming it, instantly as the GM types, and the database write follows about 0.5 s later. A field emptied, or holding spaces only, keeps what was typed, with "Untitled Event" as the greyed placeholder of an empty field, and leaving it fills nothing in; the event is saved with no name, and the queue and the log show "Untitled Event". A rename and the notes write no log entry and survive leaving the encounter and restarting the app.

Failure:

- A quit within about 500 ms of the last keystroke in the name field or the notes loses that edit, since both save through the encounter's autosave.
