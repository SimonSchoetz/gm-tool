# Encounter selection and actions

**Purpose.** On the encounter screen the GM selects queue entries, one or several, and applies HP and defeated actions to them from the sidebar's Actions tab, each action written as one combat log entry from which the queue's HP, defeated marks and removals are derived. The Data, Open points and Where to look sections for the whole encounter screen are on [Encounter queue](encounter-queue.md).

**Users.** The GM, as everywhere in the app.

## How it works

Happy path:

1. A click on a queue entry selects only it and shows it in the middle column: the entry gets the app's active highlight; when the sidebar is on the Add tab it switches to Actions, and from the Actions or the Log tab it stays where it is. A Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux; either key works on every platform) adds an unselected entry to the selection and shows it, or removes a selected one, and removing the last one leaves nothing selected; removing the shown entry leaves nothing shown, and removing another entry leaves the shown entry as it is. A Shift-click selects exactly the shown entries from the anchor to the clicked entry, both included, shows the clicked entry and deselects every other entry; the anchor is the entry last clicked without Shift, a further Shift-click ranges from the same anchor, Shift wins when Cmd or Ctrl is also held, and a range never includes a combatant hidden by Hide defeated. A Shift-click with no anchor, or with its anchor removed or hidden, selects only the clicked entry and makes it the anchor. Every one of these clicks moves the Add tab to Actions and leaves the Actions and Log tabs where they are, a Cmd-click that leaves nothing selected included. Escape clears the selection, the anchor and the shown entry, also when the same press closes a dialog, except while focus is in an editable element of the middle column (the name field, the notes, a content section, the description), where it clears none of them. Reopening the encounter starts with nothing selected and nothing shown. A combatant that is removed, or hidden by Hide defeated, leaves the selection and is no longer shown, while the others stay selected.
2. With nothing selected the Actions tab reads "Select a combatant in the queue.". With combatants selected it shows the buttons at the top: Damage, Heal, Set temp HP and Set max HP, and below those Mark defeated, Unmark defeated and Remove. Below the buttons is one row per selected combatant in queue order, each with the shown name, its HP as the queue shows it ("HP is not set. Set max HP to track it." when HP is not set) and its own amount field. With two or more combatants selected, a shared amount field sits above the buttons; with one selected there is none, and selected events never count towards the two. Selected events get no row; with only events selected the tab shows the buttons, all but Remove disabled, and the line naming the events (step 8). The tab fills the sidebar panel and only the row list scrolls, so the shared field and the buttons stay in view.
3. An empty row field shows the placeholder "Amount" and the shared field "Amount for all", and each field is wide enough to show its placeholder in full. Every amount field takes whole numbers of 0 or more, and `1e3` counts as 1000. Typing in the shared field fills every row's field with that text, and any row can then be changed on its own. Damage and Heal apply to the selected combatants whose HP is set and skip the others, and are disabled when none has HP set; Set temp HP and Set max HP apply to every selected combatant. Each of the four amount buttons is disabled while a row it applies to holds an empty or invalid amount; a skipped row's amount never blocks. Enter in a field applies nothing. After an action every row keeps its amount and the shared field its text, so the same amounts can be applied again. A row keeps its own amount while its combatant stays selected, and a combatant that joins the selection starts with the shared text. With one combatant selected its row's amount is the shared amount, so it carries over to the next single combatant selected.
4. Damage takes temp HP first and the rest from current HP, and current HP can go below 0: `4 / 7 +2` with Damage 5 becomes `1 / 7`. Heal raises current HP up to max HP, never above, and leaves temp HP as it is. Set temp HP replaces the temp HP, and 0 clears it. Set max HP changes max HP for this encounter only: when HP was not set, current HP becomes the new max; when the new max is below current HP, current HP drops to it; raising it leaves current HP as it is. The entity's stat block is never changed.
5. Mark defeated turns the image of every selected combatant that is not defeated grey whatever its HP, and Unmark defeated brings back the colours and the tint its HP gives for every selected combatant that is defeated. Mark defeated is disabled when all selected combatants are defeated and Unmark defeated when none is. No HP value sets or clears the mark.
6. Remove takes every selected item out of the queue at once, events included, without a confirmation, and clears the selection.
7. Each action writes one entry to the encounter's combat log when its button is clicked, without the autosave delay: a `damage`, `heal`, `temp-hp`, `max-hp`, `defeated`, `undefeated` or `removed` entry that lists every combatant the action changed and, where it takes one, the amount each got. A group action is one entry, so it applies to all its targets or none, and the Log tab shows it as one line, such as "Damage: Goblin 1 (5), Goblin 2 (3)". The queue's HP, defeated marks and removals are derived from those entries, so they survive leaving the encounter and restarting the app.
8. The Actions tab's rows list only the selected combatants, random combatants included, and every action works on a random combatant as on an entity combatant. When events are selected, a muted line below the rows names them: "Remove also removes: Lair action, Reinforcements". With only events selected, the tab shows the buttons, all but Remove disabled, and that line.
9. Remove takes every selected item out of the queue, events included, as one `removed` entry, and the log reads "Removed: Goblin 1, Lair action". Reverting it brings the events back at their old places with their names and notes.

Failure:

- A group action is one log write, so it applies to all its targets or to none, and a target the action cannot change is left out of its entry.
- While an action write of the encounter is pending, every action button is disabled, and stays so until the queue shows the new numbers; a second action call made in the same moment is refused and writes nothing.

Where the user waits: after an action the buttons stay disabled until the queue shows the new numbers, which is the write plus the refetch of the log.
