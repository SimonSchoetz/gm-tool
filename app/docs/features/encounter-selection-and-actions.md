# Encounter selection and actions

**Purpose.** On the encounter screen the GM selects queue entries, one or several, and applies HP and defeated actions to them from the sidebar's Actions tab, each action written as one combat log entry from which the queue's HP, defeated marks and removals are derived. The Data, Open points and Where to look sections for the whole encounter screen are on [Encounter queue](encounter-queue.md).

**Users.** The GM, as everywhere in the app.

## How it works

Happy path:

1. A click on a queue entry selects only it and shows it in the middle column: the entry gets the app's active highlight; when the sidebar is on the Add tab it switches to Actions, and from the Actions or the Log tab it stays where it is. A Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux; either key works on every platform) adds an unselected entry to the selection and shows it, or removes a selected one, and removing the last one leaves nothing selected; removing the shown entry leaves nothing shown, and removing another entry leaves the shown entry as it is. A Shift-click selects exactly the shown entries from the anchor to the clicked entry, both included, shows the clicked entry and deselects every other entry; the anchor is the entry last clicked without Shift, a further Shift-click ranges from the same anchor, Shift wins when Cmd or Ctrl is also held, and a range never includes a combatant hidden by Hide defeated. A Shift-click with no anchor, or with its anchor removed or hidden, selects only the clicked entry and makes it the anchor. Every one of these clicks moves the Add tab to Actions and leaves the Actions and Log tabs where they are, a Cmd-click that leaves nothing selected included. Escape clears the selection, the anchor and the shown entry, except while focus is in an editable element of the middle column (the name field, the notes) or while a popup or a modal dialog is open, where it clears none of them. Reopening the encounter starts with nothing selected and nothing shown. A combatant that is removed, or hidden by Hide defeated, leaves the selection and is no longer shown, while the others stay selected.
2. With nothing selected the Actions tab reads "Select a combatant in the queue.". With combatants selected it shows two sections. "HP" holds, with two or more combatants selected, the shared amount field labelled "Set for all:"; then one row per selected combatant in queue order, each with the shown name, its HP and its own amount field; then Damage and Heal side by side; then, under the caption "Set HP", the buttons "Current", "Temp" and "Max" in one row, at the small text size, with the accessible names "Set current HP", "Set temp HP" and "Set max HP". "State" holds one defeated button, labelled "Mark defeated" or "Unmark defeated" (step 5), and, below a thin divider, Remove. A row's HP line reads `cur/max`, then `+N temp` in the primary colour while temp HP is above 0, then "· defeated" while the combatant is defeated, wrapping when it needs more width; below it a small bar shows the share of max HP that is missing in red from the right, the rest near-white, and along its top, from the left, a thin primary-colour line as wide as temp HP ÷ max HP, at most the whole bar. A combatant whose HP is not set shows "HP is not set. Set max HP to track it." and no bar, and no "· defeated" even when it is defeated. Damage and Remove turn red on hover, as delete buttons do, and Heal turns green. With one combatant selected there is no shared field, and selected events never count towards the two. Selected events get no row; with only events selected the tab shows the buttons, all but Remove disabled. The tab's content starts at the top, and when it is taller than the sidebar panel the whole tab scrolls.
3. An empty row field shows the placeholder "Amount" and the shared field "Amount", and each field is wide enough to show its placeholder in full. Every amount field takes whole numbers of 0 or more, and `1e3` counts as 1000. Typing in the shared field fills every row's field with that text, and any row can then be changed on its own. Damage, Heal and Set current HP apply to the selected combatants whose HP is set and skip the others, and are disabled when none has HP set; Set temp HP and Set max HP apply to every selected combatant. Each of the five amount buttons is disabled while a row it applies to holds an empty or invalid amount; a skipped row's amount never blocks. Enter in a field applies nothing. After an action every row keeps its amount and the shared field its text, so the same amounts can be applied again. A row keeps its own amount while its combatant stays selected, and a combatant that joins the selection starts with the shared text. With one combatant selected its row's amount is the shared amount, so it carries over to the next single combatant selected.
4. Damage takes temp HP first and the rest from current HP, and current HP can go below 0: `4/7 +2` with Damage 5 becomes `1/7`. Heal raises current HP up to max HP, never above, and leaves temp HP as it is; a Heal on a defeated combatant whose HP ends above 0 also unmarks it, and one that leaves it at 0 or below keeps the mark. Set current HP sets current HP to the amount, at most max HP, and leaves temp HP as it is; it unmarks a defeated combatant when the resulting current HP is above 0, keeps the mark at 0 or below, and never marks. Set temp HP replaces the temp HP, and 0 clears it; a defeated combatant takes temp HP too and stays defeated. Set max HP changes max HP for this encounter only: when HP was not set, current HP becomes the new max; when the new max is below current HP, current HP drops to it; raising it leaves current HP as it is. The entity's stat block is never changed.
5. The defeated button reads "Mark defeated" while any selected combatant is not defeated, and applies to those; it reads "Unmark defeated" when every selected combatant is defeated, and applies to all of them. With only events selected it reads "Mark defeated" and is disabled. Mark defeated turns the combatant's image grey whatever its HP and, when HP is set, sets current HP to 0, from any value, and temp HP to 0; without HP set it only marks, and temp HP stays. Unmark defeated brings back the colours and the tint the HP gives and, when HP is set and current HP is below 1, sets it to 1; a current HP of 1 or more stays. Damage, Set temp HP and Set max HP never mark or unmark, and no HP value marks a combatant. Defeated marks and heals stored without `hpRules` ([Encounter queue](encounter-queue.md), Data) change no HP and unmark nothing, so an encounter holding them shows the HP and marks they were made with.
6. Remove opens the app's one-click confirm dialog, titled `Remove <names>` and reading `Are you sure you want to remove <names>?`, where `<names>` are the selected items' shown names, comma-separated in queue order ("Remove Goblin 1, Lair action"), with its CONFIRM button. CONFIRM takes every selected item out of the queue at once, events included, as one `removed` entry, and clears the selection; the log reads "Removed: Goblin 1, Lair action", with no Revert. Closing the dialog without CONFIRM, with Escape or a click beside it, removes nothing and keeps the selection.
7. Each action writes one entry to the encounter's combat log when its button is clicked, Remove when its CONFIRM is, without the autosave delay: a `damage`, `heal`, `current-hp`, `temp-hp`, `max-hp`, `defeated`, `undefeated` or `removed` entry that lists every combatant the action changed and, where it takes one, the amount each got. A group action is one entry, so it applies to all its targets or none, and the Log tab shows it as one line, such as "Damage: Goblin 1 (5), Goblin 2 (3)". The queue's HP, defeated marks and removals are derived from those entries, so they survive leaving the encounter and restarting the app.
8. The Actions tab's rows list only the selected combatants, random combatants included, and every action works on a random combatant as on an entity combatant.

Failure:

- A group action is one log write, so it applies to all its targets or to none, and a target the action cannot change is left out of its entry.
- While an action write of the encounter is pending, every action button is disabled, and stays so until the queue shows the new numbers; a second action call made in the same moment is refused and writes nothing.

Where the user waits: after an action the buttons stay disabled until the queue shows the new numbers, which is the write plus the refetch of the log.
